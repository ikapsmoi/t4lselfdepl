Verify dates and tickets with the festival

Before booking travel, confirm the edition dates, venue, entry requirements, ticket name, price, currency and refund or transfer conditions on the official Mysteryland website. Do not rely on old search results or assume that an event pass is part of a travel quote.

Plan the journey from India

  • Compare flight routes, arrival airport, baggage and a buffer before and after the event dates.
  • Check accommodation location, nights, room basis, local taxes and the route to the festival.
  • Confirm transfers, local transport, meals, insurance and document requirements for your nationality.
  • Keep the festival ticket separate from flights and other travel arrangements unless a written offer explicitly includes it.

Estimate the full trip in INR

Build a trip total from current, itemised amounts: official festival access + flights + accommodation + local transfers + meals + insurance + applicable visa, card and foreign-currency fees. For foreign-currency charges, use the exchange rate your payment provider will apply and include its transaction fee. Keep estimates separate from confirmed prices and add a contingency for changes.

Compare package and independent bookings

Package proposalAsk who provides each service, what dates and ticket status apply, what is included or excluded, the total price and currency, payment milestones, cancellation rules and support arrangements.
Independent bookingsPrice tickets through the official source and compare flights, accommodation and transport separately for the same dates, room basis, baggage and cancellation flexibility.
