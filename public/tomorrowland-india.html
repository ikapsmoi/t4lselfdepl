Tomorrowland from India

What to know before planning a group trip to Tomorrowland in Boom, Belgium, from festival timing to travel planning and verified availability.

Tomorrowland is one of the world's best-known electronic dance music festivals. It takes place in Boom, Belgium, and is known for large-scale stage production, multiple performance areas, DreamVille camping and an international traveler community.

Planning note: This is an editorial travel guide. It does not confirm Tomorrowland tickets, flights, accommodation or a Travel4life package. Check the current festival and travel availability before making plans.

When is Tomorrowland?

The festival story in the Travel4life collection identifies Tomorrowland as a July event held across two weekends. Dates, ticket phases and entry rules can change, so travelers should confirm the current details with the official event channels before booking flights from India.

What should travelers from India plan for?

  • International travel to Belgium and onward transport to Boom.
  • Festival entry and any accommodation or camping arrangements.
  • Travel documents, insurance and personal emergency contacts.
  • Transport between accommodation, the festival area and the airport.
  • A group communication plan if traveling with other people.

Why consider group travel?

A group trip can make the planning and social side of a major festival easier, especially for travelers attending internationally for the first time.
