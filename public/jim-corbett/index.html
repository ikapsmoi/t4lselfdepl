A practical starting sequence

Choose the trip before choosing the room.

Corbett planning is easier when the occasion, dates and group needs lead the search—not an unverified “best resort” label.

01 / DEFINE

Set the brief

Pick your date window, likely number of nights, traveller count and the reason for the trip. Note what is fixed and what can move.

02 / COMPARE

Check the stay

Ask for the exact locality, room category, occupancy, meals, taxes, cancellation terms and current availability in writing.

03 / CONNECT

Plan the details

Confirm route and transfer requirements for your own origin. Ask separately about safari permits, gates, timings and any activities; none are included in the workbook.

04 / CONFIRM

Keep one clear version

For an event or group, align the guest list, rooming, schedule, venue layout, food, transport and payment terms in a dated proposal.

Weekend, family, couple, luxury, safari and event options depend on the date and a property's verified services. This guide does not show live inventory or confirm park access, transport, venue capacity or inclusions.
