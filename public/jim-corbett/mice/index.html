Meetings, incentives, conferences & events

A strong offsite starts
before the agenda.

Travel4life can help structure a Jim Corbett group-travel enquiry around headcount, rooms, meeting format and the outcomes your team needs. The resort workbook contains no conference facilities, capacity, AV, event inventory or availability data—so every venue and service is a question to verify, not a claim on this page.

i

Venue fit is not assumed. Before a recommendation, confirm the meeting layout and capacity, dependable power and connectivity needs, AV, breakout spaces, meal timing, room configuration, outdoor-event terms, transport and a weather alternative.

The proposal audit

Make the options comparable.

Send the same brief to every shortlisted property and compare confirmed deliverables, not assumptions.

A

Space & setup

Room dimensions, seating plan, safe capacity, setup/strike time, breakout areas and rain backup.

B

Technology & support

AV inventory, technician coverage, connectivity expectations, power backup and any rental costs.

C

Stay & meals

Room types, single/double occupancy, rooming list deadlines, meal service, taxes and dietary handling.

D

People & logistics

Transfers, arrival waves, parking, accessibility, on-site coordinator, emergency contacts and group movement.

E

Activities & events

Operator, equipment, inclusions, supervision, weather rules, event permissions, sound policy and alternatives.

F

Commercial terms

Itemised quote, taxes, minimum spends, deposits, cancellation, attrition, payment dates and excluded items.

Plan with clarity

Questions worth asking.

Can I filter resorts by meeting capacity?

Not yet. The supplied list does not contain conference facilities or capacity. We will not label a resort as suitable until its current layout and capacity have been confirmed.

Does a workbook rate include a meeting room or meals?

No. The figures are per-night workbook references and do not describe occupancy, meals, AV, meeting space, tax or event inclusions.

How should I estimate room requirements?

Start with attendee count, single versus shared occupancy, arrival/departure dates and any room-category requirements. Confirm room blocks and release dates in the proposal.

Can you include transport and activities?

Include them in your brief as requested components. Route, operator, capacity, price, safety arrangements and availability all require separate confirmation.
