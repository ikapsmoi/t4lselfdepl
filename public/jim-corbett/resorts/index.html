How to use this guide

Compare the brief, not just a number.

A nightly figure can mean very different things depending on the room, number of guests, meal plan, date and taxes. Use the workbook figures as a first reference, then request an itemised quote with the same occupancy and inclusions across the options you are comparing.

The source file includes a property name, a broad category and three seasonal figures per night. It does not identify resort addresses, zones, gates, room types, amenities, safari support, photographs, wedding lawns, conference rooms or event capacity. These fields are intentionally left unclaimed until checked.

For a group celebration or team gathering, send your expected headcount, dates and room-night needs. Travel4life can help turn that brief into a shortlist request; no property on this page is represented as an available venue until its current details have been confirmed.
