What group travel can simplify

  • Shared airport or festival transfers.
  • A single trip coordinator and communication channel.
  • Rooming and arrival coordination.
  • Shared planning for documents, meeting points and emergencies.
  • Social connection for first-time international festival travellers.

What to agree before paying

Confirm whether everyone books the same dates, what happens if one traveller cancels, how rooms are allocated, whether deposits are refundable, which costs are shared and who handles passport, visa, insurance and flight changes.

Group size and flexibility

Larger groups may benefit from coordinated transport but can be less flexible. Ask whether the itinerary is fixed, whether solo travellers can join, and how changes are communicated. A useful package page should explain these choices rather than simply say “group trip”.

Travel4life role: A Travel4life group trip should state its actual host, dates, capacity, inclusions, exclusions and booking terms. It does not make Travel4life the official festival organiser.
