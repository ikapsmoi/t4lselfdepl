The cost formula

Indicative trip budget = festival or access cost + flights + accommodation + local transport + travel insurance + visa-related costs where applicable + food + optional experiences.

What to price separately

  • Festival access: confirm the official pass or package category and sales conditions.
  • Flights: compare the Indian departure city, baggage, dates and arrival airport.
  • Accommodation: price the number of nights, room basis, city tax and distance from the festival.
  • Transport: include airport transfers, festival shuttles, local rail or taxis.
  • Documents and protection: check visa, insurance and emergency-support needs.
  • Daily spending: budget for meals, deposits, connectivity and personal expenses.

Package versus independent planning

A package may reduce coordination work and add group support, but it can bundle items you do not need. Compare the named inclusions, exclusions, payment schedule and cancellation terms with an independent estimate. Never assume flights or festival access are included.

FAQs

How much does Tomorrowland cost from India?

There is no single honest price. It depends on the destination, edition, access, departure city, nights, room basis, exchange rate and inclusions.

Can I use a Travel4life listing as a quote?

Only the live listing and its written booking terms can confirm its current price and inclusions.
