Who this page is for

This page is intended for Indian travel agents, tour operators, destination resellers, corporate planners, travel trade partners and group travel businesses evaluating Tomorrowland-related package options. It is designed to support trade discussions, not to promise a fixed rate card or guaranteed inventory.

For a useful quotation, it helps to share the destination, event type, travel dates, traveller count, room configuration, departure city and the components you want quoted: flights, hotel, transfers, festival access, ground support or group coordination.

What can be discussed in a trade enquiry

  • Whole-trip planning for a Tomorrowland trip from India.
  • Group travel coordination for agents and organisers.
  • Event-specific route planning for Belgium, Winter and Thailand travel options.
  • Supplier-dependent package comparisons covering stays, transfers and travel timings.
  • Custom quote requests for a specific travel window, traveller count or accommodation requirement.

Official source information remains important for the underlying event, including the Belgium sales information and Winter festival ticket guidance.

What information helps us prepare a relevant quote

To prepare a faster, more useful response, include:

  • Your agency or company name and contact details.
  • Preferred destination or event: Belgium, Winter, Thailand or a different festival option.
  • Approximate date window and trip duration.
  • Number of travellers, room sharing preference and any group or family requirement.
  • Expected package components: flights, hotel, airport transfer, local transport or festival access.
  • Whether you need a trade quotation, availability check or package comparison.

How the B2B enquiry process works

We use the information supplied in the enquiry to understand the travel intent and package needs before confirming whether a relevant supplier option or product support is available. Availability, rates and terms depend on the live offer and booking conditions at the time of the request.

We may ask for additional details on group size, accommodation requirements, travel window, payment schedule or support expectations before finalising a quotation. We do not publish confidential supplier terms or rates that are not approved for public display.

Frequently asked B2B questions

Do you offer public rate cards?

Not always. Public pricing may be withheld when rates are supplier-dependent, event-specific or commercially sensitive. The best approach is to request a trade quote with current trip details.

Is this an official Tomorrowland partner page?

No. This page is informational and commercial-support oriented. It is not an official Tomorrowland partnership page and should not be interpreted as a claim of organiser status or guaranteed access.

Can I request a package for a larger group?

Yes. Group travel, agent enquiries and custom arrangement requests are all appropriate enquiry types. Clear traveller counts, room configurations and timelines help prepare a more useful quotation.
