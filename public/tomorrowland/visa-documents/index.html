Important: Visa and entry requirements depend on destination, nationality, passport and travel dates. This page is a preparation checklist, not legal or immigration advice. Verify current requirements with official authorities.

Documents to organise

  • Valid passport with the required validity and blank pages.
  • Visa or entry permission where applicable, applied for through the official process.
  • Flight, accommodation, festival-access and transfer confirmations.
  • Travel insurance policy, emergency contacts and medical information.
  • Copies stored securely offline and online, without sharing unnecessary personal data.

Match documents to the actual trip

The destination matters: Belgium, France for Tomorrowland Winter and Thailand have different entry processes. Your package provider may help with an itinerary or supporting booking records, but the traveller remains responsible for checking and meeting official requirements.

Before departure

Check name spelling, dates, passport details, insurance exclusions, payment records and the emergency contact for your group. Leave copies with a trusted contact and keep access to the original documents secure.
