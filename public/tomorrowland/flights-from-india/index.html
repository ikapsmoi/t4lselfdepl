Flight planning from India

Flights from India to Tomorrowland

Flight planning is more than finding the lowest fare. Build enough time for connections, immigration, baggage, transfers and a calm arrival before the festival.

Choose the flight plan around the festival

Start with the destination and official dates, then compare departure cities, connection airports, baggage, arrival time and the cost of reaching your accommodation. Avoid an itinerary that requires a same-day rush to the festival.

Questions for a package enquiry

  • Which Indian departure cities are supported?
  • Are flights included, optional or excluded?
  • Which airline, baggage allowance and fare conditions apply?
  • What happens if the flight changes or is delayed?
  • Who arranges airport, hotel and festival transfers?

Plan arrival buffers

Include time for long-haul connections, entry formalities, baggage collection and onward transport. If you are travelling with a group, agree a meeting point and a backup plan before departure.

Prices change: This guide does not publish a fixed flight price. Use current airline or travel-provider data for a quote, and read the cancellation and change conditions.
