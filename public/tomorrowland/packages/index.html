What a genuine package should specify

A useful Tomorrowland package proposal should name the destination and edition it covers, state its exact travel dates, and separate festival access from transport and stay arrangements. Do not assume that a flight, hotel, transfer or festival pass is included simply because it appears in a headline.

  • Edition, weekend, dates, departure city and trip duration.
  • Festival access: included, excluded, or separately purchased, with the seller identified.
  • Accommodation name and location, number of nights, room basis and meal terms.
  • Flight status, baggage, transfers, local transport and any independent arrangements.
  • Total price and currency, taxes, deposit, payment schedule and cancellation terms.
  • Named organiser, traveller support contact, exclusions and emergency process.

Questions to ask before booking

Ask for an itemised written itinerary and confirm what happens if a flight changes, a transfer is delayed or an included service is unavailable. Check whether the seller is responsible for each component and which parts are provided by third parties. Compare the terms with the festival’s own published sales information; the festival’s brand does not itself verify an independent travel seller.

Before requesting options, have your preferred destination, departure city, group size, room preference and approximate trip length ready. Ask for current availability and a dated quote rather than relying on an old social post, search snippet or an undated “from” price.

How to assess a Travel4life listing

Only an active product listing can establish Travel4life’s own dates, price, availability and inclusions. Review its published conditions and ask for written confirmation of any detail that is missing. If no relevant listing is available, treat this page as planning guidance, not proof that a package is currently on sale.

Festival schedules and sales conditions can change. Use the official Tomorrowland website for current event information and independently verify the seller, payment recipient and cancellation policy before making a booking.
