Plan travel from India

  • Compare flight routes and arrival times only after confirming the official event dates.
  • Check the hotel location, room basis, number of nights and the transport plan to the festival.
  • Confirm whether festival access, airport transfers, festival shuttles, baggage and insurance are included or separate.
  • Check current entry, passport and visa requirements with official authorities for your nationality and travel dates.
  • Keep the provider's written price, currency, taxes, exclusions, payment dates and cancellation terms.

Separate event access from travel services

A festival pass, hotel, flight and local transfer are different parts of a trip. Do not assume that a travel-provider enquiry includes an official pass. A genuine product page or written booking document should name every included service, identify the responsible provider and state what remains for the traveller to arrange.
