Where and when are the Tomorrowland editions?

The following status was checked against the linked official pages on 24 October 2026. Sale inventory can change. If an official page or checkout changes, follow the organiser's latest information rather than this dated summary.

Tomorrowland Belgium

The festival takes place in Boom, Belgium. The official Belgium welcome page lists the 2026 weekends as 17–19 July and 24–26 July and marks that edition as concluded. The official pages reviewed do not confirm Belgium 2027 dates or a ticket-sale schedule. Check the official Belgium site and its sales information for future announcements.

Tomorrowland Winter in Alpe d'Huez, France

The official site lists 20–27 March 2027. It says 7-, 4- and 1-day festival tickets are on sale; a festival ticket includes general festival access and a lift and ski pass. The 4- and 7-day packages are marked sold out. The official package description includes accommodation, a lift and ski pass, and festival access; transport or Discover Paris can be added. Confirm current stock and exact terms on the official Winter site before booking.

Tomorrowland Thailand

The official site lists 11–13 December 2026 at Wisdom Valley, Thailand, and states that the 2026 event is sold out. The official page reviewed does not announce a later edition. Check the official Thailand page for any change or future announcement.

Tomorrowland Brasil

The official site lists 30 April–2 May 2027 in Itu, São Paulo. As checked on 24 October 2026, it says Saturday and Full Madness passes are sold out, Friday and Sunday tickets or remaining DreamVille packages may be available, and Global Journey packages are sold out. Availability can change; verify the exact option and total on the official Brasil site. Brasil is included here as ticket information; this site does not currently have a dedicated Brasil travel page.

How do you check official Tomorrowland ticket availability?

Ticket categories, sale windows, prices and availability are set for each edition. Start with the official site for the destination you want, then confirm the current sale status and full terms in its ticket checkout. The status summaries above are a dated guide, not live inventory.

  1. Choose an edition and follow its official site from the links above.
  2. Check the ticket category, named benefits, total price, currency, fees and any personalisation or payment deadlines.
  3. Use the organiser's own checkout and read its current transfer, resale and cancellation rules before paying.
  4. Only then commit to flights or accommodation with dates and cancellation terms that work for your plans.

What is the difference between a Tomorrowland ticket and a travel package?

A festival ticket grants only the access and other benefits stated by the organiser for that specific ticket. A travel package is a separate offer whose written terms should name each included service, such as transport or accommodation. Do not assume that a package includes a festival ticket, or that a travel-only booking will provide access to an event that is sold out.

Before paying a travel provider, request the responsible provider's name, an itemised itinerary, ticket source and status (if a ticket is explicitly included), total price and currency, payment schedule, exclusions, cancellation terms and booking confirmation process. Avoid unauthorised resale offers and never treat a request form or WhatsApp draft as a completed booking.

Where should you stay for Tomorrowland?

Compare accommodation by its address, travel time to the relevant venue, the transport available after the festival, number of nights and cancellation terms. Do not assume that a room booking includes festival entry or a transfer. Use the Tomorrowland accommodation checklist to compare stay options, and check official practical information for the edition before choosing a route.

Tomorrowland ticket FAQs

Can I buy Tomorrowland tickets through Travel4life?

No. Travel4life is an independent travel provider, not the festival organiser or an authorised ticket seller. Use the official edition site for ticket purchases and availability.

Does a Tomorrowland travel package include a festival ticket?

Only when the specific provider's written offer explicitly names the ticket, its source, status and terms. A travel enquiry or travel-only booking does not provide festival access.

When do Tomorrowland Belgium tickets go on sale?

The official Belgium pages reviewed on 24 October 2026 do not confirm a 2027 date or ticket-sale schedule. Check the official Belgium site and its sales information for announcements.

Can I buy Tomorrowland tickets at the venue?

This guide cannot confirm on-site ticket availability. Do not plan around a gate sale; use the relevant official edition site to verify whether any tickets are available and how they can be purchased.

Can I buy or sell a Tomorrowland ticket through a resale listing?

Transfer and resale rules depend on the edition and ticket type. Read the current official terms and use only channels the organiser identifies; an unofficial listing is not proof that a ticket is valid or transferable.
