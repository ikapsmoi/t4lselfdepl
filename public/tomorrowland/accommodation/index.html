Accommodation choices

  • Festival camping or immersive stays: confirm what is included, arrival rules, facilities and the exact number of nights.
  • Hotel stays: check the property, distance, room type, breakfast, taxes and festival transfer plan.
  • Winter accommodation: ask how close the stay is to Alpe d'Huez activities and whether ski-related services are bundled.
  • Thailand hotel packages: confirm hotel, shuttle, pass terms and any additional Thailand itinerary.

The details a package should disclose

Look for the accommodation name or category, check-in and check-out dates, room basis, occupancy, meals, deposits, city taxes, transport, accessibility and cancellation terms. “Hotel included” is not enough information to compare offers.

Location versus convenience

A central hotel may simplify restaurants and independent travel, while an official or nearby festival stay may reduce transfer coordination. Choose based on your group, budget and tolerance for late-night travel.

Do not assume: Accommodation does not automatically include festival access, flights, transfers, meals or insurance. Confirm each item in writing.
