What a package may offer

One provider may coordinate accommodation, transfers, group communication, itinerary support and selected travel services. The value depends on what is named, current and useful to you. A package may also be less flexible or cost more than booking each component yourself.

What independent planning may offer

Booking separately can give you more choice over flights, hotels, dates and daily plans. It also means you manage connections, changes, documents, transfers, refunds and communication across multiple suppliers.

Compare the same items

  • Festival access: included, excluded or separate?
  • Flights: included, optional or self-booked?
  • Accommodation: exact property, nights, room basis and taxes?
  • Transport: airport, festival and local transfers?
  • Support: named contact and emergency process?
  • Money: total price, deposit, payment schedule and cancellation?
Best comparison method: Write down the complete independent cost first, then compare it with the package's complete written inclusions and exclusions. Do not compare a headline price with an incomplete DIY estimate.
