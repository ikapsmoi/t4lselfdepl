What a genuine product page should show

  • Named Tomorrowland edition and weekend or travel dates.
  • Whether festival access is included, excluded or purchased separately.
  • Accommodation name, location, nights, room basis and meal terms.
  • Flight status, departure options, transfers and local transport.
  • Price, currency, taxes, deposit, payment schedule and cancellation policy.
  • Traveller support, emergency contact and what the traveller arranges independently.

Send a useful enquiry

Include your preferred edition, departure city, group size, room preference, desired trip length and whether you need flights or only accommodation and local coordination. The response should be specific enough to compare against independent planning.
