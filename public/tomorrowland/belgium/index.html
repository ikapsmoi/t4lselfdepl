Build the journey from India

  • Compare flight routes, baggage rules, connection times and arrival buffers for your actual travel dates.
  • Confirm the accommodation address, number of nights, room basis and the transport plan to and from the festival.
  • Check current passport, visa and insurance requirements with the relevant official authorities.
  • Keep festival access separate from hotel, flights, transfers and other services while comparing offers.
  • Save the named provider, itinerary, payment schedule, exclusions and cancellation terms in writing.

What a travel package should make clear

Before booking any travel service, ask whether festival access is included or must be purchased separately. Request the named accommodation, travel dates, transport arrangements, taxes and fees, support contact, payment milestones and cancellation conditions. Do not infer a pass, room or flight from a package headline.

Compare the full Belgium trip budget

Set out international flights, accommodation, local transport, festival access, meals, insurance, baggage and applicable fees as separate line items. Check the lodging address and onward transport against your itinerary, and verify the event dates and access directly with Tomorrowland. The final total depends on the dates, providers and services selected; no fixed package price is claimed here.

How to ask about Belgium travel options

  1. Share your intended edition or travel dates, departure city and number of travellers.
  2. Explain whether you need help checking flights, accommodation, local transport or other arrangements.
  3. Before booking, request written confirmation of the responsible providers, itinerary, pass status, inclusions, exclusions, total price, payment schedule and cancellation terms.

Travel is not festival access: This enquiry does not provide or guarantee official passes or entry. Treat flights, stays, transfers and event access as separate unless written provider terms explicitly include them.
