What a Belgium travel package may cover

  • International flights from an Indian departure city, if explicitly included.
  • Accommodation in a specified hotel, apartment or camping arrangement.
  • Airport, city and festival-area transfers where stated.
  • Travel coordination, group support and an itinerary with named dates.
  • Festival access only when the individual product explicitly includes it.

How to evaluate a Tomorrowland Belgium package

Ask for the exact edition and weekend, accommodation name, room basis, transfer plan, flight status, festival-access terms, visa responsibility, insurance, taxes, payment milestones and cancellation policy. A product page should be specific enough for you to compare it with planning independently.

Where Indian travellers usually need clarity

Allow time for the international journey, arrival in Belgium, local transport to Boom and the return transfer. Check passport and visa requirements with the relevant authorities, and confirm the official festival sales information before paying for travel.

FAQs

Does every Belgium package include a Tomorrowland pass?

No. Confirm this on the individual product or enquiry response. Travel and festival access are separate components unless explicitly bundled.

Where is Tomorrowland Belgium held?

The official festival site identifies Boom, Belgium. Local accommodation and transfer details vary by package.
