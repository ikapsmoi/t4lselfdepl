Why France works well for long weekends and full trips

Paris is usually the strongest starting point for a first Europe trip, and it pairs naturally with Switzerland, Belgium or nearby western Europe destinations. For travellers who value city landmarks, food and culture, France offers a strong mix of convenience and recognisable experiences.
