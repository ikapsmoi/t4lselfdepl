Why travellers choose Switzerland

Switzerland works especially well for scenic, relaxed and premium first-time Europe holidays. It is a natural fit for couples, families and travellers who want a comfortable rhythm with lakes, mountains and efficient rail connections.

Popular route combinations

  • Paris + Switzerland
  • Switzerland + Italy

Best fit

Choose Switzerland if you want scenic stays, premium mountain settings and a route that feels relaxed rather than overly packed.
