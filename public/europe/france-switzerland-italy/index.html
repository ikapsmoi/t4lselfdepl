Typical route logic

Travellers often start in Paris, move into Switzerland for scenic stays, then continue into Italy for culture, food and historical city time. This route appeals to those who want an iconic Europe snapshot without trying to cover too many countries in a short amount of time.

Recommended duration

  • 10-14 days for a comfortable multi-city trip
  • 7-day shorter versions are possible when focusing on fewer city stays
