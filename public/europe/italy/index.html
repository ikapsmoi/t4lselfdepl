What makes Italy a strong Europe pick

Italy offers a combination of famous landmarks, comfortable rail connectivity and an easy structure for first-time travellers. It is often paired with Switzerland for scenic balance, or with France for a west-to-east route across Europe.

Good route fits

  • Italy + Switzerland
  • France + Switzerland + Italy
  • Rome + Venice + Milan or Florence combinations
