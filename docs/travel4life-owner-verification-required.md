# Travel4Life owner verification required

This checklist records business facts and operational claims that still need evidence or an explicit owner decision. A missing verification record is not evidence that a claim is false. Do not restore a claim to public copy until its owner, source, scope and review date are recorded.

## Customer proof and marketplace claims

- [ ] Provide the source, calculation, population and reporting period for any public rating or traveler-count metric.
- [ ] For each proposed testimonial or review, record the original customer statement, trip attribution, publication permission and any edits. Confirm that displayed ratings are calculated only from eligible, authentic reviews.
- [ ] Confirm whether creator/host verification means identity, experience, credentials or another defined check. Document the actual process and avoid implying checks that are not performed.
- [ ] Verify any comparative or superlative wording, including “India's most trusted,” and provide current, representative evidence before use.

## Product-specific commercial terms

- [ ] Establish an authoritative owner/source for each trip's currency, price basis, taxes and fees, supplier, availability timestamp, deposit, payment schedule and included/optional/excluded services.
- [ ] Confirm cancellation, refund, amendment and minimum-group terms per product and supplier. Do not substitute global defaults when trip-specific terms are absent.
- [ ] Confirm the meaning and evidence basis of capacity, booking counts, availability and urgency statements before displaying them.
- [ ] Verify any host earnings, fees, payout, commission or savings claims against current documented terms and a reproducible calculation.

## Safety, insurance and support

- [ ] Confirm exactly which trips include insurance, who underwrites it, coverage limits, exclusions and how a traveler receives the policy.
- [ ] Document the actual emergency escalation process, staffed hours, responsible provider and reachable contacts. Confirm whether any 24/7 support claim is accurate.
- [ ] Verify whether GPS sharing, buddy matching, background checks or other safety features exist, are enabled, and are described with appropriate limitations.
- [ ] Distinguish Travel4Life-operated support from supplier/host responsibilities in customer-facing copy.

## Tomorrowland, suppliers and business relationships

- [ ] Confirm current supplier availability and authorization for any Tomorrowland-related pass, accommodation, transport or package offer. Record the source and validity period.
- [ ] Confirm whether any official-organizer affiliation, partner status, ticket access or resale/transfer authorization may be claimed. Do not imply official status without written permission.
- [ ] Define what “Buy Back Packages” means, who provides the service, applicable transfer/resale terms, and the evidence supporting the claim.
- [ ] Confirm the B2B role Travel4Life can actually perform, what can be quoted, who receives agent enquiries, the response process, and whether any data is retained.

## Company identity, credentials and contact

- [ ] Confirm the registered legal entity name, approved public address/location and current canonical contact details.
- [ ] Verify founding-date, GST and MSME statements against current records and approve the exact public wording.
- [ ] Verify permission and current relationship for each named partner, logo or endorsement.
- [ ] Approve the README and public-site contact details independently; repository copy is not itself proof of business ownership or contact availability.

## Enquiry and consent handling

- [ ] Decide whether newsletter signup is a real opt-in or only a prepared email draft. For an opt-in, identify the consent record, confirmation, unsubscribe and retention mechanisms.
- [ ] For WhatsApp drafts, confirm the configured recipient, fields copied, user disclosure and that preparing/opening a draft is not reported as a sent message, submitted lead or booking.
- [ ] Set a data-minimization and retention policy for any future B2B lead form or CRM flow. Do not add persistence until the owner has approved the purpose and notice.
