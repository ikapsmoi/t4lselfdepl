- undisclosed cash usage for booking thailand packages
- any search that asks for how to keep cash travel payments hidden from tax authorities

- Link from `/help` and the support section to `/cash-travel-guide`.

- Keep the page neutral: informative, compliant, and precise about when to seek official airline, agency or tax guidance.

- This is a search-intent map, not a legal guarantee. Official airline policies, tax law, and KYC rules remain the final authority for any specific case.
- The page intentionally avoids recommending undeclared cash or transaction splitting, because that would not be a legitimate or compliant travel-support content strategy.
