# Tomorrowland Authority and Trust Plan

**Reviewed:** 24 October 2026

## Positioning

Travel4life can earn trust through transparent, useful travel planning for people travelling from India. It must not present itself as the Tomorrowland organiser, official partner, authorised ticket seller, or source of guaranteed access unless the owner obtains independently verifiable authorisation.

## Editorial work

1. Maintain a dated official-status summary for Belgium, Winter, Thailand and relevant announced editions such as Brasil.
2. Publish practical India-origin checklists for flights, connection buffers, lodging location, onward transport, baggage, insurance and quote comparison.
3. Keep a ticket-versus-travel-package explainer that itemises what a pass, a travel package and a Travel4life enquiry do and do not mean.
4. Create original destination comparisons and trip-budget worksheets based on live written quotes, without stale currency tables.
5. Update dates, sale states and links when official pages change; mark unknown status as unknown rather than inferring.
6. Have an identified editor/owner review time-sensitive status at least monthly while ticket sales are active and before each campaign.

## Legitimate external authority

- Seek genuine editorial coverage from travel, music and India-origin travel publications where a useful original guide or expert contribution fits.
- Offer original planning assets, such as a festival-trip cost checklist or a comparison of official ticket versus travel-package components.
- Build real relationships with licensed travel providers and publish partner claims only with written approval and a verifiable listing.
- Use owner-approved brand profiles and accurate business information. Do not manufacture reviews, credentials, backlinks, partnerships or customer stories.
- Contact communities only with relevant information and without automated or repetitive promotional posting.

## Trust and conversion requirements

- Every current event or sales claim links to the official source and shows a review date.
- Any Travel4life product page must state the responsible provider, ticket status, price validity, inclusions, exclusions and cancellation terms.
- A WhatsApp draft is not a received enquiry; a quote is not a booking; a travel booking is not a festival ticket.
- Keep analytics free of personal enquiry contents and honor existing privacy controls.

## Measurement

Measure organic landing-page traffic, official-source clicks and contact-action clicks separately. Track qualified enquiries only through an authorized, privacy-compliant CRM or reporting process. Do not treat visits, WhatsApp opens or draft creation as leads or sales.
