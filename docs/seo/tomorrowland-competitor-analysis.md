# Tomorrowland Competitor and Search-Result Analysis

**Reviewed:** 24 October 2026
**Scope:** Search-result sampling and direct review of authoritative edition sources. No claim of Travel4life rankings is made.

## Search-result sample

Bing result pages were requested for:

- `Tomorrowland tickets from India package`
- `Tomorrowland Winter 2027 tickets packages`
- `Tomorrowland Thailand packages from India`
- `Tomorrowland Belgium tickets travel package India`

The returned result sets were nearly identical and predominantly generic (Tomorrowland home, Wikipedia, YouTube and broad event results), rather than the requested edition- and India-specific queries. This sample is not reliable evidence of Google ranking positions, query-specific competitors, or commercial search demand. No SERP position or competitor traffic is asserted.

The 23 October Search Console workbook has query rows but no query × landing-page cross-tab. It cannot identify which competing domains appeared beside Travel4life for those rows.

These are authoritative information competitors, not a verified list of commercial domains ranking for the target queries. The direct official pages were retrieved on 24 October 2026. Their status may change.

## Travel4life content gaps closed

- No single central page previously owned broad ticket-information intent separately from package enquiries. `/tomorrowland/tickets/` now owns that intent.
- Edition-specific status and package inclusion claims now cite the relevant official source and review date.
- Unverified Belgium, Winter, Thailand and Brasil numeric pass-price tables and stale currency conversions were removed from the public guide pages.
- The cost guide now explains how to build a budget from current official checkout details and live supplier quotes.
- The Thailand product-card insertion is disabled while the official 2026 event is sold out and ticket inclusions remain unverified; no data-layer changes were made.
