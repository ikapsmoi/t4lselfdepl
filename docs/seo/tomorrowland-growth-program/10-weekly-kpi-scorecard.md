## Commercial outcomes

Report only qualified enquiries with supported source attribution. Do not count link opens or WhatsApp drafts as confirmed leads.

## Alert thresholds

- 20% or greater priority-page click loss for two weeks.
- 30% or greater impression decline without seasonality explanation.
- Priority URL disappears from Search Console coverage.
- Canonical or indexing regression.
- Query moves to the wrong landing page.
- Priority query enters position 10 or improves by five positions sustained over 28 days.
